Sleeping Beauty Mountain

Located in the Lake George Wild Forest of the Adirondacks, featuring diverse ecology, including my personal favorite tree of the area, Quaking Aspen.

Indian Medows Nature Preserve

The Town of Glenville's wetland preserve that follows the banks of the Indian Kill, with eleven species of ferns!

Overlook Mountain

The southernmost peak if the Catskill Escarpment in the Central Catskill Mountains, a stone's throw away from Woodstock, New York.

Mount Rainer National Park

The highest peak of the Cascades with acres galore of old-growth forests and wildflower meadows.
